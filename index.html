Real Estate Social Content — 3-Pillar Framework

Six-slide carousel strategy helping real estate brands move beyond basic property listings to build trust and leads.

View Case Study →

Brief: Created a six-slide strategic carousel for real estate and lifestyle brands aiming to turn passive social media viewers into qualified leads.

Concept: Structured around three pillars—educating buyers first, humanizing the brand with behind-the-scenes and neighborhood stories, and selling lifestyle outcomes paired with matching content formats.

Result: Delivered a scannable, conversion-focused carousel framework with a clear summary matrix and DM call to action to showcase strategic content planning.
